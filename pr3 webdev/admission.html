Admission Information

Know the admission process and eligibility criteria.

Admission Process

Admission is based on the eligibility criteria of the selected course. Students who satisfy the required qualifications should visit the admission office with the necessary documents. After document verification and fee payment, admission will be confirmed by the college.

Eligibility

Students must satisfy any one of the following:

~ Minimum 90% Marks in Class 12 Board Examination

OR

~ ACPC Rank above 10000

Required Documents

~ 10th Marksheet

~ 12th Marksheet

~ School Leaving Certificate

~ Aadhaar Card

~ Passport Size Photographs

~ Category Certificate (If Applicable)
